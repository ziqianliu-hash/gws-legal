Ground We Share — Support

Help for the free iOS app · Developer: Ziqian Liu

Find what you have in common: movies, music artists, countries, and relationship boundaries.

Contact us

For questions, problems, or privacy requests, email admin@sceneswemet.com.

For a technical issue, include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. If you attach a screenshot, hide private information. Please do not send your private selections or invitation and reply text.

How do two people find an overlap?

  1. The sender chooses a topic and items, then copies and sends the invitation text through a messaging service.
  2. The responder imports the invitation, makes their own selections, and sends back the generated reply text.
  3. The original sender imports the reply on the device holding that round to compute the overlap.

In this version, only the sender receives the result in the app.

Why won't a reply work?

Check that you copied the complete reply without editing it and are returning to the original round on the original device. A reply from another round will not work. Replacing a pending round or removing its local data may prevent the original reply from being processed.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Searching the bundled lists and computing matches work locally. Your messaging service, external links, and this support website may require internet access.

Where are my results?

Completed sender results and responder reply summaries are stored locally, with optional private round notes. Notes are not included in invitation or reply messages or result images. Records remain until you delete them; there is no automatic ten-record limit. The app has no developer-operated account backup or sync service. We cannot recover missing local rounds or results. Device backups depend on your Apple settings.

How do I remove data?

Delete individual records or all history in the app. Deleting history preserves pending comparisons; deleting a completed record also clears its saved result display when applicable. When available on the result screen, "Start over" clears the current session.

To stop waiting, open the current comparison and choose "Abandon this comparison" After confirmation, a late reply can no longer complete that comparison. Pending comparisons do not automatically expire. Earlier device backups and messages or images you shared must be managed separately.

半分知己 — 支持

免费 iOS 版使用帮助 · 开发者：Ziqian Liu

发现彼此在电影、音乐艺人、国家和关系边界上的共同点。

联系我们

使用问题、故障或隐私请求，请发邮件至 admin@sceneswemet.com。

报告故障时，请附上应用版本、iPhone 或 iPad 型号、iOS 版本及复现步骤。截图请遮盖私人信息，不要发送私人选择清单、邀请或回传文字。

两个人怎样计算交集？

  1. 发起方选择场景及条目，将生成的邀请文字通过聊天服务发送给对方。
  2. 响应方导入邀请，完成自己的选择，再把生成的回传文字发回。
  3. 原发起方在保存该轮次的原设备上导入回传，计算交集。

当前版本仅发起方在应用中获得结果。

为什么无法处理回传？

请确认复制了完整回传文字、没有修改内容，并回到原设备上的原轮次。其他轮次的回传无法使用。替换待完成轮次或移除本地数据后，原回传可能无法继续处理。

需要账号或联网吗？

不需要账号。搜索内置词表和计算交集在本地完成。聊天服务、外部链接及本支持网站可能需要联网。

结果保存在哪里？

发起方的已完成结果、回复方的已回复摘要及可选的私人轮次备注保存在本机。备注不进入邀请、回传消息或结果图片。记录保留到你主动删除，不再自动限制为十条。没有开发者运营的账号备份或同步服务，我们无法恢复丢失的本地轮次或结果。设备备份取决于你的苹果设置。

怎样删除数据？

你可以在应用中删除单条记录或全部历史。删除历史会保留等待中的轮次；删除已完成记录时，也会在适用时清除它对应的结果展示副本。结果页提供「重新开始」时，可用它清除当前会话。

如果不想继续等待，打开当前轮次，选择「放弃这一轮」。确认后，即使收到晚到的回传，也无法再计算这一轮的结果。待完成轮次不会自动过期。旧设备备份及已分享的消息、图片需要另行管理。
